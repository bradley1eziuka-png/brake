Privacy Policy

How Brake Painting & Home Improvement handles the information you share with us. Plain language, no fine print.

Effective date: August 10, 2026

Brake Painting & Home Improvement LLC ("Brake Painting," "we," "us") keeps this simple. We only collect what we need to give you an estimate and do the work. This page explains what we collect and how we handle it.

What we collect

When you fill out the estimate form on this site, you share:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • Your address or ZIP code
  • Details about your project

We collect this only when you choose to send it. We don't track you across other websites or run advertising cookies.

How we use it

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your estimate request
  • Schedule and complete your project
  • Contact you about the work you asked about

That's the whole list. We don't use your details for anything beyond serving you.

How the form is processed

Our estimate form runs through Formspree, a third-party form service. When you submit the form, your information passes through Formspree's servers on the way to our inbox. Formspree processes that data so we can receive your request. You can read how they handle it in Formspree's privacy policy.

We don't sell or share your information

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with third parties for their marketing. The only place your information goes is to us, through Formspree as described above, so we can respond to you.

Access or delete your information

Want to know what we have on file, or want us to delete it? Email brakepainting@gmail.com and we'll take care of it.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we'll change the effective date at the top of this page.

Questions

Reach us anytime. Call 734-624-2930 or email brakepainting@gmail.com.
