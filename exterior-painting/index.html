Exterior House Painting

A repaint that protects, not just covers

A good exterior paint job starts long before the first coat. We prep the surface, fix what's failing, and use coatings that stand up to Michigan's freeze-thaw swings.

Peeling paint and faded siding are usually a sign the surface underneath needs attention. We wash away dirt and chalking, scrape and sand loose paint, and caulk the gaps where water sneaks in. Where we find soft or rotted wood, we repair it first, because paint over rot just hides a problem that keeps spreading. See our wood rot repair & trim carpentry if your trim, fascia, or siding needs work before the repaint.

From there we prime bare spots, protect your windows, plants, and fixtures, and lay down even coats of quality exterior paint. The result holds its color and keeps moisture out for years, not months.

Our Process

How an exterior job runs

Same steps every time, whether it's a single-story ranch or a full two-story elevation.

  1. 1

    Walkthrough & written estimate

    We look at the whole exterior, talk through colors, and put a clear scope and price in writing.

  2. 2

    Prep & repair

    Wash, scrape, sand, and caulk. We fix rotted wood and worn trim before any paint goes on.

  3. 3

    Prime & protect

    Prime bare wood and repairs, then cover plants, windows, walkways, and fixtures.

  4. 4

    Paint & final walkthrough

    Even coats of quality paint, a clean job site, and a walkthrough with you before we call it done.
