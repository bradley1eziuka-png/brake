Interior Painting

Fresh rooms, clean lines, no mess

From a single room to a whole-home refresh, we protect your space, prep it right, and leave you with crisp, even color.

We paint living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens, dining rooms, hallways, stairwells, and home offices. Along with walls and ceilings, we handle trim, baseboards, doors, and accent walls, so every surface in the room ties together.

Interior work lives and dies on prep. We move and cover furniture, protect your floors, patch nail holes and cracks, sand rough spots, and caulk gaps before we ever open a can. The payoff is straight lines, an even finish, and a room that looks finished the day we leave.

Color Help

Not sure on color? We'll help.

Picking a color you'll love for years is the hard part. Jerry walks you through it and shows you how it reads in your space before you commit.

Bring us a vibe or a Pinterest board, or start from scratch. We help you weigh undertones, sheen, and how the light in each room changes a color through the day. Once you've settled on a direction, you'll know what to expect before the first coat goes on. Our Process

How an interior job runs

  1. 1

    Walkthrough & color help

    We look at the rooms, talk color and sheen, and put a clear scope in writing.

  2. 2

    Protect & prep

    Cover floors and furniture, patch and sand, and caulk gaps for a smooth base.

  3. 3

    Prime & paint

    Prime where needed, then cut clean lines and roll even coats on walls and ceilings.

  4. 4

    Final walkthrough & cleanup

    We touch up, put your room back together, and leave it cleaner than we found it.
